Imam Husain ibn Ali (a.s.)

The grandson of Prophet Muhammad (S.A.W.S.) and son of Imam Ali (a.s.) and Bibi Fatema al-Zahra (a.s.). He stood alone against the armies of Yazid at the plains of Karbala on 10 Muharram 61 AH, sacrificing his life to uphold the truth of Islam — "Hayhaat minna zilla" (Never shall we accept humiliation).

🕌

Ashara Mubaraka

The ten sacred days of Muharram observed by the Dawoodi Bohra community in the presence of Syedna Mufaddal Saifuddin (T.U.S.), the 53rd Dai al-Mutlaq. Mumineen gather from across the world to hear his bayaan, to weep for Imam Husain (a.s.), and to renew their bond with the Ahlul Bayt.

✦

The Azam (Pledge)

During Ashara, every Mumin makes a firm Niyyat — an intention to dedicate these days entirely to Imam Husain (a.s.). Closing worldly affairs, seeking leave from work and studies, performing khidmat, observing Shariat, and giving voice to Noha and Aweel are acts of love for the Imam.
